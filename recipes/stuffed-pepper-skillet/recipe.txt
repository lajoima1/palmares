name: Stuffed Pepper Skillet
description: A skillet version of classic stuffed peppers with savory Italian sausage, tender rice, tomatoes, and melty Colby Jack cheese, all in one pan.
servings: 6 servings (≈1 ½ cups each)
prep_time: 20 minutes
cook_time: 20 minutes
total_time: 40 minutes
difficulty: Easy
tags: pork, rice, main, weeknight

ingredients:
- 1 lb (≈450 g) ground Italian sausage
- 1 medium onion, diced (≈1 cup)
- 3 bell peppers, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup long grain rice, uncooked
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 can (≈411 g / 14.5 oz) diced tomatoes (with juices)
- 1 can (≈227 g / 8 oz) tomato sauce
- 1 cup shredded Colby Jack cheese
- 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

instructions:
1. Brown the Italian sausage in a large deep skillet over medium heat for 5-6 minutes, breaking it apart as it cooks. Drain excess fat if needed.
2. Add the onion, bell peppers, and garlic. Cook 4-5 minutes until softened.
3. Stir in the rice, Italian seasoning, and smoked paprika. Cook 1 minute to lightly toast the rice.
4. Add the beef broth, diced tomatoes with their juices, and tomato sauce. Scrape the pan bottom to release the browned bits.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer 18-20 minutes, until the rice is tender and the liquid is mostly absorbed.
6. Remove from heat, sprinkle the cheese on top, and cover 2-3 minutes to melt.
7. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

notes:
- Sweet, mild, or hot Italian sausage all work well.
- To substitute ground beef, chicken, turkey, or pork, increase the Italian seasoning to 2 tsp and season with salt and pepper.
- Use long grain white rice for proper liquid absorption, and keep the lid on during simmering so the rice cooks via steam.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3 days and freeze up to 1 month.
