name: Unstuffed Bell Peppers
description: All the ingredients and flavor of stuffed bell peppers cooked in one skillet for an easy, free-form bowl meal.
servings: 6 servings (≈1 ⅓ cups each)
prep_time: 15 minutes
cook_time: 45 minutes
total_time: 1 hour
difficulty: Easy
tags: beef, rice, main, weeknight

ingredients:
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 yellow onion
- 2 bell peppers
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ lb (≈225 g) ground beef
- 1 can (≈425 g / 15 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup long grain white rice, uncooked
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 ½ cups beef broth
- 1 can (≈227 g / 8 oz) tomato sauce
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- 1 Tbsp chopped parsley (optional garnish)

instructions:
1. Mince the garlic; dice the onion and bell peppers.
2. Add the olive oil and ground beef to a large deep skillet over medium heat and brown completely.
3. Add the diced vegetables, garlic, basil, oregano, and pepper. Stir until the onion softens.
4. Add the diced tomatoes with their juices, uncooked rice, and beef broth. Stir briefly.
5. Cover the skillet and increase heat to medium-high until the broth boils. Reduce to low and simmer 15 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat and let rest, covered, for 5 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, combine the tomato sauce with the Worcestershire sauce.
8. Remove the lid and fluff the rice with a fork, gently folding the ingredients together.
9. Pour the tomato sauce mixture over the rice and sprinkle with mozzarella.
10. Cover and let the residual heat melt the cheese. Garnish with parsley and serve.

notes:
- Do not substitute cauliflower rice (it releases too much liquid).
- Freezes well; store in individual portions up to 3 months.
- Use heavy, thick cookware for even cooking.
